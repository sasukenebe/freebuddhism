Status: published and technically verified; full human proofreading remains pending.
This is not a new text-license policy and does not approve full human proofreading.

- Preserve the November 11 permission policy, original text contents, original
  homepage and Git history. Do not modify the audio or video projects.
- Make file-specific reuse terms conspicuous in populated ZIP downloads. Keep
  each work's original restrictions; never apply a blanket NC or CC0 license.
- Correct stale empty-archive labels and redundant copyright presentation while
  retaining every complete original permission notice and distinct attribution.

  the validated release using normal fast-forward history. Deploy only FreeBuddhism.

  regressions for every confirmed wrong target and recompare real publisher HTML.

  metadata. CC0/public-domain files must not acquire neighboring restrictions.
