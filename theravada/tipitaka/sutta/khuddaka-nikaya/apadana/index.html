Apadana

Verse accounts of past deeds associated with monks and nuns.

Jonathan S. Walters’s licensed English translation covers all 601 available SuttaCentral Apadāna pages: Tha Ap 1–561 and Thi Ap 1–40.

The pinned Pāli edition has two additional IDs: Tha Ap 562 (Upavāna), a parallel version of the story translated at Tha Ap 24, and Tha Ap 563 (Raṭṭhapāla), parallel to Tha Ap 20. Their Pāli verses differ, so these links identify related story versions, not exact translations of IDs 562–563.
