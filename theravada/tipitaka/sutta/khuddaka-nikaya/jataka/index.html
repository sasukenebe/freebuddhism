Khuddaka Nikaya

Jataka

Canonical verses about the Buddha’s previous lives, preserved with their traditional commentary stories in a complete six-volume English translation.

All 547 numbered Jātaka articles are available in Cowell et al.’s six-volume English edition (1895–1907). Some articles refer to other stories or omit passages; the edition is not always self-contained. Separate verse translations appear where available.

Browse Jātaka stories by topic.
