Khuddaka Nikaya

The Piṭaka-Disclosure (Peṭakopadesa)

A work on interpreting and organizing the Buddha’s teaching; included in some Theravāda editions.

This work is edition-specific and is not included in every Theravāda Khuddaka list.

The complete source text and scan are not included here because their CC BY-NC license does not meet the CC0/public-domain policy.
