111. “Bhikkhus, while considering the external factors that impel or drive a person, I do not know of a single thing that is more supportive in the amount of good it brings to oneself, than associating with and having genuinely good spiritual friends.

“For truly, associating with and having genuinely good spiritual friends brings you much good.”
