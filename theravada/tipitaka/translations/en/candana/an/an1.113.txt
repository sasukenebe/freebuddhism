113. “Bhikkhus, while considering the internal factors that impel or drive a person, I do not know of a single thing that is more constructive in the amount of good it brings to oneself, than having the desire for and the pursuit of good and beneficial habits.

“For truly, having the desire for and the pursuit of good and beneficial habits brings you much good.”
