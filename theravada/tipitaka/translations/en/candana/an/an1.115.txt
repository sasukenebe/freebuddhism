115. “But, Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more protective of This Beautiful and Good Dhamma, promoting its healthy continuity, non-degeneration and its non-disappearance, than living with heedfulness, as a result of which the essence of the Teachings is kept alive and undeterred, where there is no confusion or uncertainty in the hearts and minds of beings, who then come to even attain Its highest Fruits.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, because of living with heedfulness, This Beautiful and Good Dhamma will continue to thrive, and those who taste Its Fruits will continue to flourish.”
