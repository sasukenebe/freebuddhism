130. “Bhikkhus, those Bhikkhus who explain what is adhamma as Dhamma, are not just harming themselves, but bringing much harm and pain onto so many others as well, causing the suffering of many people. In doing so, they are ruining not just their own lives, but the lives of many

others, creating misfortune and miserable merit-making and damaging results not just to their own detriment, but to the detriment of all others, both Devas and humans.

“Therefore, these Bhikkhus are bringing harm and misery not just to themselves, but to countless others, leading to the rapid degeneration and inevitable disappearance of This Beautiful and Good Dhamma.”
