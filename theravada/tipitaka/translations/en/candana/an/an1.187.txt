187. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of any person throughout existence, who is able to maintain and keep turning This Matchless Wheel of Dhamma, set in motion by The Tathāgata, as does Sāriputta!

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, Sāriputta truly keeps maintaining This Matchless Wheel of Dhamma, that was set in motion by The Tathāgata.”
