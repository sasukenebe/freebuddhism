20. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of even a single thing that so prevents and eliminates the arising of skeptical doubt which has not yet arisen, and evaporates the skeptical doubt that has already arisen in the heart, as the application of wise radical attention.

“Therefore, it is no surprise, Bhikkhus, that whoever trustingly applies wise radical attention, will surely experience the prevention of the not yet arisen skeptical doubts, and thus avert them from arising, as well as the evaporation of skeptical doubt that has already arisen.”
