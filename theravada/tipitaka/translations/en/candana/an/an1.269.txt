269. “Bhikkhus, it is simply impossible for someone who has finally attained to Right View, to ever look upon anything that is put together and thus conditioned (saṅkhārā), and yet continue to believe that it could be truly pleasurable and satisfying. There is absolutely no possibility for this to occur.

“However, it is quite possible that an ordinary unlearned person should look upon things that are put together and conditioned (saṅkhārā), and see them as truly pleasurable and satisfying. There is the possibility for this to occur.”
