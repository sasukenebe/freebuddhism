275. “Bhikkhus, it is simply impossible for someone who has finally attained to Right View, to ever try and maliciously create a division within The Sangha. There is absolutely no possibility for this to occur.

“However, it is quite possible that an ordinary unlearned person could try and maliciously create a division within The Sangha. There is the possibility for this to occur.”
