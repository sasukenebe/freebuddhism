307. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more comforting than Right View, for it leads to the arising of beneficial qualities which were thus far absent, while at the same time fanning the flames of those good and beneficial qualities that had already manifested and were present, as it further intensifies them, making them grow.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, when there is Right View in your hearts, then good and beneficial qualities will soon arise, and whatever good and beneficial qualities that are already present will simply intensify and grow further, because of your Right View.”
