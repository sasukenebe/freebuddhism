309. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more comforting than Right View, for it leads to the arising of good and beneficial qualities which were thus far absent, while at the same time wilting away all the evil and damaging qualities that were already present.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, when there is Right View in your hearts, then it will bring forth good and beneficial qualities within you, and obliterate any evil and damaging qualities you might have had.”
