311. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more life-enhancing than wise radical attention, for it leads to the arising of Right View in you, which was thus far absent, while at the same time encouraging the growth of any Right View that may have already been present, increasing and making it even stronger.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, when there is wise radical attention, then it will lead to the arising of Right View within your hearts, while establishing and strengthening the already present Right View within you, even more.”
