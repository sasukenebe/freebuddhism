313. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more soothingly reassuring than being established in Right View, for it is the reason whereby beings find themselves reappearing into delightful states, when at their moment of death, once the body breaks down, they are instantaneously reborn into happy destinations, reappearing in the Divine realms of the Devas, to experience unimaginable enjoyments.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, because of being established in Right View, when their bodies break down, right after the moment of death, beings are reborn into happy states, as they immediately reappear into heavenly destinations, in the Divine realms of the Devas, to experience unimaginable enjoyments.”
