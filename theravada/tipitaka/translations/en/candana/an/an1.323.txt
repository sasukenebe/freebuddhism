323. “Bhikkhus, while discussing a well-proclaimed teaching, the one expounding or relating it to others must exercise much caution and scrutiny while delivering his message, for that is again not the responsibility of the recipient of that information, who is listening to what is being taught to him.

“And what is the reason for this?

“Bhikkhus, it is because the teaching being discussed is itself well-proclaimed and deeply multilayered.”
