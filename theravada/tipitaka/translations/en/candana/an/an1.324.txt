324. “Bhikkhus, while dedicating himself with persevering effort in a badly proclaimed teaching and training, one will inevitably come to much suffering, sooner or later.

“And what is the reason for this?

“Bhikkhus, it is because the teaching being followed is itself wrong and badly proclaimed.”
