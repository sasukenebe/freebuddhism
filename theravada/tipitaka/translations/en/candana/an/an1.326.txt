326. “Bhikkhus, being lazy and not applying any effort in a badly proclaimed teaching and training, one will come to much happiness and relief, sooner or later.

“And what is the reason for this?

“Bhikkhus, it is because the teaching is itself wrong to begin with, and badly proclaimed; therefore, the lazy person will find himself neither being challenged nor confronted for his wrong attitude.”
