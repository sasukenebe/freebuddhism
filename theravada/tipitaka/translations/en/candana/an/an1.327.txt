327. “Bhikkhus, while dedicating himself with persevering effort in a well proclaimed teaching and training, one will inevitably come to much happiness.

“And what is the reason for this?

“Bhikkhus, it is because the teaching being discussed is itself well-proclaimed and deeply multilayered.”
