336. “The same can be said, Bhikkhus, about those beings who are wise, considerate, and intelligent, who are able to understand the meaning of what is being said, those with the ability to discern while scrutinizing the good teaching from the one that is evil.

“For, indeed, such beings are truly very few, when compared to the countless other beings who are dense, stubbornly pig-headed and thick, arrogant and dull-witted fools; those mindless ones who are unable to understand the meaning of what is being said, let alone discern by distinguishing the Good Teaching from the one that is evil.”
