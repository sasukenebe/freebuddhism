337. “In the same way, Bhikkhus, those beings who possess the Noble Eye of Wisdom are indeed very few, when compared to the countless other beings who are lost, being deeply immersed in ignorant confusion; loitering in their cyclical existences, and trapped endlessly within the clutches of saṃsāra.”
