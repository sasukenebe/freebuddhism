340. “The same can be said, Bhikkhus, about those beings who, on hearing The Dhamma taught by The Tathāgata, retain it by keeping it close to their hearts, for such beings are truly but a few in number, when compared to the vast number of beings who neither retain nor keep The Dhamma close to their hearts.”
