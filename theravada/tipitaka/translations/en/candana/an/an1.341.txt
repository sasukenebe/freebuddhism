341. “Similarly, Bhikkhus, those beings who on hearing The Dhamma taught by The Tathāgata, examine the meaning of what has been retained and kept close in their hearts, are truly few in number, when compared to the countless number of beings, who are not interested in examining the meaning of The Dhamma that was somehow retained.”
