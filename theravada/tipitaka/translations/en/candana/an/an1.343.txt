343. “And, Bhikkhus, those beings who muster up the sense of urgency within their hearts towards things that cannot be delayed or postponed, are indeed too few when compared to the countless others, who continue procrastinating as they live without a sense of urgency in regards to matters that should neither be delayed nor postponed.”
