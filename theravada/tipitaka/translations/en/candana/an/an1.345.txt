345. “The same applies, Bhikkhus, in the case of those beings who drop and move away from those things that do not matter, and set themselves squarely upon The Deathless, undeterred in their commitment and trajectory, becoming firmly established while focusing on the Heart’s Release as their samādhi deepens, for these beings are truly few in number when compared to those innumerable beings who do not gain any samādhi, let alone become resolute in the attainment of The Deathless.”
