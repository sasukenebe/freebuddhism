39. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that leads to so much harm as an untamed, unguarded, uncontrolled, unprotected, and unrestrained mind. For, a mind that is untamed, unguarded, uncontrolled, unprotected, and unrestrained is truly harmful.
