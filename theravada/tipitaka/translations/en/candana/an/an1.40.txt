40. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that leads to so much happiness and ease, as a mind that is tamed, guarded, controlled, protected, and restrained. For, a mind that is tamed, guarded, controlled, protected, and restrained is truly beneficial, as it brings much good.”
