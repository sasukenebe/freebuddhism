47. “Bhikkhus, among all trees that are pliable and wieldy, the sandalwood is the chief, given its softness and adaptability to pressure and pliability.

“In the same manner, Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is as adaptable in its pliability as the mind, once it is properly developed and disciplined. This, because the well-developed mind that is properly disciplined, is indeed pliable and wieldy.”
