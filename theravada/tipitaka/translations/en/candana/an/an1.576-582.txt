576. “Bhikkhus, there is that one single thing, which when you cultivate to perfection, returning back to it again and again, it will spiral into and directly lead you to the full maturation and pinnacle of your sense of urgency.

“And what is that one single thing?

“It is mindfulness firmly anchored upon the body. Therefore, Bhikkhus, when you cultivate mindfulness of the body, by constantly directing and anchoring it upon the body, returning back to it again and again, it will spiral into and directly lead you to the full maturation and pinnacle of your sense of urgency.”

581. “Bhikkhus, there is that one single thing, which when you cultivate to perfection, returning back to it again and again, it will spiral into and directly lead you to live and continue experiencing a state of soothing happiness throughout, right here, in this very life.

582. “Bhikkhus, there is that one single thing, which when you cultivate to perfection, by returning back it to again and again, it will spiral into and directly lead you to the attainment of the Fruit of Perfect Release and the Certainty in Directly Knowing that you are now finally Released!

“And what is that one single thing?

“It is mindfulness firmly anchored upon the body. Therefore, Bhikkhus, when you cultivate mindfulness of the body, by constantly directing and anchoring it upon the body, by returning back to it again and again, it will spiral into and directly lead you to the attainment of the Fruit of Perfect Release and the Certainty in Directly Knowing that you are now finally Released!”
