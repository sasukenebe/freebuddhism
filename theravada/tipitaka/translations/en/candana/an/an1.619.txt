619. “Bhikkhus, all those who fail in their efforts to observe the mind by establishing it on the body, are the ones who fail in ever experiencing The Deathless!

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, firmly anchor your mindfulness upon the body, for it will most certainly take you straight to The Deathless!”
