620. “Bhikkhus, all those who remain heedless and inattentive by their neglect of the mind, as it goes on being disconnected from the body, are the ones who fail in ever experiencing The Deathless!

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, firmly anchor your mindfulness upon the body, for it will most certainly take you straight to The Deathless!”
