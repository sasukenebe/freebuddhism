75. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more formidable than possessing wise radical attention, as a result of which the Factors of Awakening that may have not yet arisen, will awaken and grow exponentially, and thus come to be fully developed and reach their potential of accomplishment.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, by living with wise radical attention, the unarisen Factors of Awakening will surely come to their full development and fruition.”
