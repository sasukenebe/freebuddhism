79. “Having much wealth is ultimately meaningless, Bhikkhus. What you should strive to increase is not the size of your riches, properties, or possessions, but your wisdom!

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, you should focus all your energy on increasing the wisdom in your heart! That is how you must train yourselves!”
