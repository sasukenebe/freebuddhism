81. “Having much renown or fame is meaningless, Bhikkhus. What you should strive to increase is not your fame, how well you are known, or the popularity you possess. Instead, you should strive to increase your wisdom!

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, you should focus all your energy on increasing the wisdom in your heart! That is how you must train yourselves!”
