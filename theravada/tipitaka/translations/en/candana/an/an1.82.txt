AN I

Suttas 82-97

Paṭhama Pamādādi Vagga “On Careless Neglect – Part 1”

82. “Bhikkhus, I do not know of a single thing that is more insidious while causing so much harm, than living with careless neglect.

“Careless neglect truly will bring you much harm.”
