AN I

Suttas 98-139

Dutiya Pamādādi Vagga “On Careless Neglect – Part 2”

98.

“Bhikkhus, while considering the internal factors that impel or drive a person, I do not know of a single thing that is more insidious in the amount of harm it inflicts, than living with careless neglect.

“For truly, careless neglect brings you much harm.”
