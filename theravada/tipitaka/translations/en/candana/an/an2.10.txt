10. Vassūpanāyika Sutta (Observing the Rains Retreat) “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of observing the Rains. Whish two?

“Observing the earlier Rains retreat and observing the later Rains, wherein the latter of these two begins a month after the first has ended.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, these are the two ways of observing the Rains.”
