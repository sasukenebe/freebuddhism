104. “Bhikkhus, these are the two kinds of fools. What two?

“The one who considers what is adhamma to be The True Dhamma, while the other looks at what in fact is The True Dhamma, but sees it as adhamma.

“These are the two kinds of fools, Bhikkhus.”
