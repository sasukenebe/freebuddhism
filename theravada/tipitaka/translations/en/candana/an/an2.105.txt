105. “Bhikkhus, these are the two kinds of considerate and wise people. What two?

“The one who considers what is adhamma as indeed not The Dhamma, and the other considers what in fact is The True Dhamma, to be none other than The True Dhamma, indeed.

“These are the two kinds of considerate and wise people, Bhikkhus.”
