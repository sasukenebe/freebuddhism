106. “Bhikkhus, these are the two kinds of fools. What two?

“The one who considers what is not the Discipline to be the Discipline (Vinaya), and the other who considers what really is the Discipline, but sees it as not being the Discipline.

“These are the two kinds of fools, Bhikkhus.”
