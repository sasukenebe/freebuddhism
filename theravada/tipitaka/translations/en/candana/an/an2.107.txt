107. “Bhikkhus, these are the two kinds of considerate and wise people. What two?

“The one who considers what is not the Discipline as not being the Discipline (Vinaya), and the other considers what really is the Discipline, as none other than the Discipline.

“These are the two kinds of considerate and wise people, Bhikkhus.”
