108. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants (āsavas) continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who becomes deeply concerned and feels remorse over things for which there is no need, and the other who does not become deeply concerned nor feels remorse over things, for which he truly needs to become deeply concerned and worried.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants (āsavas) continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
