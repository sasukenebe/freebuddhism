109. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants (āsavas) do not continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who does not become deeply concerned nor feels remorse over things for which there is no need, and the other who becomes deeply concerned and feels remorse over things for which there is a need.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants (āsavas) do not continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
