110. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers that which is not allowed as something allowed, and the one who considers what in fact is allowed, as something not allowed.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
