111. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants do not continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers that which is not allowed as something not allowed, and the one who considers what is allowed as something that is allowed.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants do not continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
