112. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers what is not an offense to be an offense, while the other considers what in fact is an offense to not be an offense.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
