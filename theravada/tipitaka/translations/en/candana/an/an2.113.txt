113. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants do not continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers what is not an offense as not being an offense, while the other considers what is an offense to indeed be an offense.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants do not continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
