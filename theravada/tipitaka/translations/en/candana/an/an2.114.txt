114. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers what is adhamma to be The True Dhamma, and the other considers what in fact is The True Dhamma yet sees it as adhamma.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
