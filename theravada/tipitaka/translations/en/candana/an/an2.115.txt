115. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants do not continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers what is adhamma as indeed not being The Dhamma, and the other considers what in fact is The True Dhamma to be none other than The True Dhamma, indeed.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants do not continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
