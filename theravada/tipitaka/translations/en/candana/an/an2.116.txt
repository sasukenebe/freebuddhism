116. “Bhikkhus, the heart’s contaminants continue to increase for these two individuals. What two?

“The one who considers what is not the Discipline to be the Discipline, and the other who considers what really is the Discipline as not being the Discipline.

“These are the two individuals for whom the heart’s contaminants continue to increase, Bhikkhus.”
