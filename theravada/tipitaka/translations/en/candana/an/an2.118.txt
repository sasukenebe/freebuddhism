AN II

Suttas 118-129

Āsāduppajaha Vagga “Wishes that are Difficult to Abandon”

118. “Bhikkhus, it is difficult to abandon these two wishes from the heart. Which two?

“The wish for more wealth, and the wish to live a long life.

“These are the two wishes that are difficult to abandon, Bhikkhus.”
