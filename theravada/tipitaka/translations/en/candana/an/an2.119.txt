119. “Bhikkhus, these two types of people are truly rare in existence. What two?

“The first is the person who takes the initiative and helps those in need without missing the opportunity, and the second is the grateful person, who reciprocates without a moment’s hesitation, while being full of appreciation.

“These are the two types of people who are truly rare in existence, Bhikkhus.”
