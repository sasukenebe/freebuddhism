121. “Bhikkhus, these two types of people are difficult to satisfy. What two?

“The one who is addicted to accumulating wealth, and the one who is addicted to recklessly wasting away whatever he gains.

“These are the two types of people who are difficult to satisfy, Bhikkhus.”
