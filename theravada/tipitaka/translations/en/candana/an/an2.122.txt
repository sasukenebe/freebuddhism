122. “Bhikkhus, these two types of people are quite easy to satisfy. What two?

“The one who is not addicted to accumulating wealth, and the one who does not recklessly waste away whatever he gains.

“These are the two types of people who are quite easy to satisfy, Bhikkhus.”
