124. “Bhikkhus, these are the two prerequisites for the arising of hatred and anger. Which two?

“The first is that which is experienced as something displeasing, with resistance towards it, while the other is having unwise and careless attention.

“These are the two prerequisites for the arising of hatred and anger, Bhikkhus.”
