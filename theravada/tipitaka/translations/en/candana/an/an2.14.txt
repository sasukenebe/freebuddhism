14. “Bhikkhus, these are the two methods of teaching The Dhamma as imparted by The Tathāgata. What two?

“Dhamma that is presented and taught in its brief form, and Dhamma that is presented and taught in detail.

“These, Bhikkhus, are therefore the methods of teaching The Dhamma as imparted by The Tathāgata.”
