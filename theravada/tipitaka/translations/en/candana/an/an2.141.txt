AN II

Suttas 141-150

Dāna Vagga “The Section on Generosity”

141. “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of being generous. What are these two?

“Being generous with the offering of material gifts, and being generous with offering the gift of The Dhamma.

“Between these two ways of being generous, being generous by offering the gift of The Dhamma is truly supreme!”
