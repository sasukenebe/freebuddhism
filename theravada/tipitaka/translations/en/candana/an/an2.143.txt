143. “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of giving away what one has. What are these two?

“The giving away of material possessions, and the giving away of The Dhamma one possesses in the heart.

“Between these two ways of giving away what one possesses, the giving away of The Dhamma one possesses in the heart is truly supreme!”
