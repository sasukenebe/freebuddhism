144. “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of giving up what was held on to. What are these two?

“The giving up of material possessions that was held on to, and the giving up of The Dhamma one was holding on to.

“Between these two ways of giving up what was held on to, giving up The Dhamma that one held on to is truly supreme!”
