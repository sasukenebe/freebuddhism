149. “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of providing support and assistance. Which two?

“Providing support and assistance with wealth and material things, and providing support and assistance with The Dhamma.

“Between these two types of providing support and assistance, providing support and assistance with The Dhamma is truly supreme!”
