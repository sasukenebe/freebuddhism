152. “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of being hospitable. Which two?

“Being hospitable with the worldly or material resources one has, and being hospitable with The Dhamma in one’s heart.

“Between these two, being hospitable with The Dhamma in one’s heart is indeed the supreme.”
