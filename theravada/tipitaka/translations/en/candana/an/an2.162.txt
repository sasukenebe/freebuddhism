162. “Bhikkhus, there are these two ways of ‘becoming fully accomplished.’ What two?

“Becoming accomplished in material or worldly things, and becoming fully accomplished in The Dhamma.

“Between these two, becoming fully accomplished in The Dhamma is truly the most supreme.”
