AN II

Suttas 163-179

Samāpatti Vagga “The Section on the Meditative Attainments”

163. “Bhikkhus, there are these two complimentary qualities. What two?

“Knowing how to successfully enter into meditative attainments, and knowing how to successfully emerge out of those meditative attainments.

“These are the two complimentary qualities, Bhikkhus.”
