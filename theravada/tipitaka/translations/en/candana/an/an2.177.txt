177. “Bhikkhus, there are these two complimentary qualities. What two?

“Not becoming contented and thus satisfied with the beneficial qualities that are present in oneself, and untiringly striving to develop further in This Holy Life.

“These are the two complimentary qualities, Bhikkhus.”
