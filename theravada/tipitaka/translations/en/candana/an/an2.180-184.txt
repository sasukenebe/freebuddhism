AN II

Suttas 180-229

Kodhapeyyālam “Abbreviated Sections, Starting with Anger”

180. “Bhikkhus, there are these two qualities. Which two?

“Anger, and a heart that is clouded with bitter resentment.

“These are the two qualities, Bhikkhus.”

181. “Bhikkhus, there are these two qualities. Which two?

“Belittling while vilifying others, and living with contempt in one’s heart.

“These are the two qualities, Bhikkhus.”

182. “Bhikkhus, there are these two qualities. Which two?

“Living with jealousy, and being stingy.

“These are the two qualities, Bhikkhus.”

183. “Bhikkhus, there are these two qualities. Which two?

“Being deceitful, and sneakiness.

“These are the two qualities, Bhikkhus.”

184. “Bhikkhus, there are these two qualities. Which two?

“Neither having wise inner moral shame, nor having wise moral fear in one’s heart.

“These are the two qualities, Bhikkhus.”
