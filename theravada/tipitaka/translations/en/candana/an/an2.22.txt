22. “Bhikkhus, these two individuals come to falsely represent The Tathāgata, by clouding His words. Which two?

“The resentful and contentiously wicked person, full of anger and hatred in his heart; and the person who blindly believes, without any proper understanding.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, these two individuals come to falsely represent The Tathāgata by clouding His words.
