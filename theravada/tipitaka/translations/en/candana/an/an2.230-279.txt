AN II

Suttas 230-279

Akusalapeyyālam “Abbreviated Section on the Harmful and Damaging”

230. “Bhikkhus, there are these two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin. Which two?

“Anger, and a heart that is clouded with bitter resentment.

“These are the two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin, Bhikkhus.”

231. “Bhikkhus, there are these two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin. Which two?

“Belittling while vilifying others, and living with contempt in one’s heart.

“These are the two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin, Bhikkhus.”

232. “Bhikkhus, there are these two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin. Which two?

“Living with jealousy, and being stingy.

“These are the two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin, Bhikkhus.”

233. “Bhikkhus, there are these two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin. Which two?

“Being deceitful, and sneaky.

“These are the two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin, Bhikkhus.”

234. “Bhikkhus, there are these two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin. Which two?

“Not having any sense of wise inner moral shame, nor wise moral fear in one’s heart.

“These are the two harmful and damaging qualities that lead to ruin, Bhikkhus.”

235. “Bhikkhus, there are these two beneficial qualities that lead to growth. Which two?

“Becoming released from anger, and liberating the heart from its once bitter resentment.

“These are the two beneficial qualities that lead to growth, Bhikkhus.”

236. “Bhikkhus, there are these two beneficial qualities that lead to growth. Which two?

“Becoming liberated from the push to belittle or vilify others, and living with a heart that is freed from contempt.

“These are the two beneficial qualities that lead to growth, Bhikkhus.”

237. “Bhikkhus, there are these two beneficial qualities that lead to growth. Which two?

“Getting released from living with jealousy, and finally being liberated from stinginess.

“These are the two beneficial qualities that lead to growth, Bhikkhus.”

238. “Bhikkhus, there are these two beneficial and good merit-making qualities that lead to growth. Which two?

“No longer being deceitful, and freedom from sneakiness.

“These are the two beneficial and good merit-making qualities that lead to growth, Bhikkhus.”

239. “Bhikkhus, there are these two beneficial and good merit-making qualities that lead to growth. Which two?
