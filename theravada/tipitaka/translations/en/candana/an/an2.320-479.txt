320-479.

a. “Bhikkhus, to fully and deeply understand reality, one has to recognize and know hatred for what it truly is. Furthermore, to thoroughly destroy hatred, for the complete giving up of hatred, for the ending of hatred, for the wiping out of hatred, for the fading away of hatred, for becoming dispassionate towards all things pertaining to hatred, for the cessation of hatred, for the abandoning of hatred, and for the relinquishing of all hatred, and for good, these two qualities should be fully developed and cultivated.

“And what are these two?

“Serene Mental Quietude (samatha), and Penetrative Insight (vipassana).

“Therefore, to fully and deeply understand reality, one has to recognize and know hatred for what it truly is. Furthermore, to thoroughly destroy hatred, for the complete giving up of hatred, for the ending of hatred, for the wiping out of hatred, for the fading away of hatred, for becoming dispassionate towards all things pertaining to hatred, for the cessation of hatred, for the abandoning of hatred, and for the relinquishing of all hatred, and for good, these two qualities you must fully develop.”
