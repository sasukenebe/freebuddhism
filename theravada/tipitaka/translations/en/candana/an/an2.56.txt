56. “Bhikkhus, there are these two types of Buddhas. Who are the two?

“One is The Tathāgata, The Arahant, The Perfectly Self-Awakened Buddha; and the other is the Paccekabuddha, who is a silent or non-teaching Buddha.

“Bhikkhus, these are the two types of Buddhas.”
