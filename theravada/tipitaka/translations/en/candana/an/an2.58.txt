58. “Bhikkhus, a loud and an earthshaking thunder bolt does not frighten these two beings. Which two?

“One is a Bhikkhu who has destroyed his heart’s contaminants (āsavas); and the other is a thoroughbred horse.

“Therefore, Bhikkhus, a loud and an earthshaking thunder bolt does not frighten these two beings.”
