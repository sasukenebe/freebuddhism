AN II

Suttas 64-76

Sukha Vagga “The Section on Happiness”

64. “Bhikkhus, happiness is of two types. What two?

“The happiness one experiences as a householder, and the happiness that is experienced by one who has gone forth.

“And among these two, Bhikkhus, the happiness that is experienced by one who has gone forth is indeed the greatest.”
