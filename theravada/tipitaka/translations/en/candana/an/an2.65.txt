65. “Bhikkhus, happiness is of two types. What two?

“The happiness one experiences from sensual pursuits, and the happiness that is experienced by renouncing and giving up sensuality altogether.

“And among these two, Bhikkhus, the happiness that is experienced by renouncing and giving up sensuality altogether is indeed the greatest.”
