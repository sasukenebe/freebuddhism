67. “Bhikkhus, happiness is of two types. What two?

“The happiness that is contaminated, and the happiness that is uncontaminated and thus free from all the āsavas.

“And among these two, Bhikkhus, the happiness that is uncontaminated and thus free from all the āsavas, is indeed the greatest.”
