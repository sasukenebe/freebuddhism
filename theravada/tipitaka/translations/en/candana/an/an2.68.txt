68. “Bhikkhus, happiness is of two types. What two?

“Material or worldly happiness connected with the body, and spiritual happiness which has nothing to do with the world nor the body.

“And among these two, Bhikkhus, the spiritual happiness which has nothing to do with the world nor the body, is indeed the greatest.”
