7. Kaṇha Sutta (Dark and Vile Things) “Bhikkhus, there are these two dark and vile things. What two?

“Lacking wise inner moral shame, and lacking wise moral fear in one’s heart.

“Bhikkhus, these are the two things that are truly dark and vile.”
