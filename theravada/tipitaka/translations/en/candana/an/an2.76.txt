76. “Bhikkhus, happiness is of two types. What two?

“The happiness that results from experiencing tangible objects of form, and the happiness that results from experiencing the nontangible and the formless.

“And among these two, Bhikkhus, the happiness that results from experiencing the nontangible and the formless is indeed the greatest.”
