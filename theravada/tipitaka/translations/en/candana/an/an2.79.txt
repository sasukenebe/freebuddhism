79. “Bhikkhus, evil and damaging qualities arise in the first place, because they themselves have a cause, and are not without one, for they need it in order to persist.

“Therefore, when that cause itself is destroyed and removed, those evil and damaging qualities will naturally fade away and occur no more.”
