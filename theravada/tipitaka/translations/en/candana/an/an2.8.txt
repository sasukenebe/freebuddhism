8. Sukka Sutta (Bright and Good Things) “Bhikkhus, there are these two bright and good things. What two?

“Possessing wise inner moral shame (hirī), and possessing wise moral fear (ottappa) in one’s heart.

“Bhikkhus, these are the two things that are truly bright and good.”
