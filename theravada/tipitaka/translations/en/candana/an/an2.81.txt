81. “Bhikkhus, evil and damaging qualities arise in the first place, because they themselves have their conditioning reasons, and thus cannot be or even arise without those conditions.

“Therefore, when those conditioning reasons are destroyed and removed, those evil and damaging qualities will naturally fade away and occur no more.”
