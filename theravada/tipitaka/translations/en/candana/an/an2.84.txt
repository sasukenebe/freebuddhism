84. “Bhikkhus, evil and damaging qualities arise in the first place, because they themselves rely on perceptions, and cannot be or even arise without the presence of perceptions.

“Therefore, when those perceptions are removed, those evil and damaging qualities will naturally fade away and occur no more.”
