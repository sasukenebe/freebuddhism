AN II

Suttas 98-117

Bāla Vagga “The Second Section on Fools”

98. “Bhikkhus, these are the two kinds of fools. What two?

“The one who takes ownership (and responsibility) for something he has not done yet, and the one who does not take ownership (and the responsibility) for what he has already done.

“These are the two kinds of fools, Bhikkhus.”
