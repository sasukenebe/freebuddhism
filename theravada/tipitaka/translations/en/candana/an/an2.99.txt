99. “Bhikkhus, these are the two kinds of considerate and wise people. What two?

“The one who does not take ownership (or responsibility) for something he has not done yet, and the one who does take ownership (and the responsibility) for what he has already done.

“These are the two kinds of considerate and wise people, Bhikkhus.”
