153. Vandanā Sutta (Homage) Bhikkhus, these are the three ways to pay homage. What three?

One pays homage through one’s bodily actions.

One pays homage through his communication and in the way he speaks.

And one pays homage through his mental actions, in the way he thinks and ponders, while engaging in thought.

Bhikkhus, these are the three ways to pay homage.
