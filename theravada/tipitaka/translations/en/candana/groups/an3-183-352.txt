AN III

Suttas 167-352

Rāga Peyyāla Vagga “The Section on Abbreviated Repetitions, Starting with Passion”

167 - 352. a. Bhikkhus, to thoroughly and directly know passion, three things should be developed. What three?

The collectedness of mind through emptiness; the collectedness of mind through sign-less-ness; and the collectedness of mind through desire-less-ness. Bhikkhus, to thoroughly and directly know passion, these three should be developed.

Bhikkhus, for the complete understanding of passion, its exhaustion, dispelling, destruction, fading away, disenchantment, cessation, and giving up of passion, these three things should be developed.
