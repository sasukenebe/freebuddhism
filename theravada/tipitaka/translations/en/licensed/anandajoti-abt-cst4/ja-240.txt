Verse 1 — Pali source
1. Sabbo jano hiṁsito Piṅgalena,
Tasmiṁ mate paccayā vedayanti,
Piyo nu te āsi akaṇhanetto?
Kasmā nŭ tvaṁ rodasi dvārapālā ti?

Verse 1 — English
All people were harassed by Piṅgala, now, because he is dead, they feel delight, what was the one with tawny eyes to you? Why are you crying, watchman of the door?

Verse 2 — Pali source
2. Na me piyo āsi akaṇhanetto,
Bhāyāmi paccāgamanāya tassa,
Ito gato hiṁseyya Maccurājaṁ,
So hiṁsito āneyya punā idhā ti.

Verse 2 — English
The one with tawny eyes wasn’t dear to me, but his coming back I truly do fear, going from here should he harass Death’s King, being harassed he may sent him back here.

Verse 3 — Pali source
3. Daḍḍho vāhasahassehi, sitto ghaṭasatehi so,
Parikkhatā ca sā bhūmi, mā bhāyi, nāgamissatī ti!

Verse 3 — English
He’s burned by thousands of cartloads, cooled by hundreds of water pots, the earth has been dug up, please do not fear, he is not coming back!

Source note 3.1 (English)
For reasons that are not clear neither of these verses has a commentary, although they would seem to require a paraphrase at the least! Note that the 3rd verse is extra to the two verses required in this section.
