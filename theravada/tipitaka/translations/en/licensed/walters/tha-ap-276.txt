Therāpadāna

The Legends of the Theras

274. Abbhañjanadāyaka

Walt 2595-2596 Unguent was given by me
to Koṇḍañña, the Blessed One,
Free of Passion, the Neutral One,
Whose Mind was as Wide as the Sky,
Non-Delayed One, Meditator,
Turner-back of All Delusion,
the Well-Wisher of Every World,
the Biped Lord, Neutral One. Verse 1-2

Walt 2597 During aeons beyond measure
since I gave Buddha unguent,
I’ve come to know no bad rebirth:
the fruit of giving unguent. Verse 3

Walt 2598 In the fifteenth aeon ago
there was a ruler, Cirappa,
a wheel-turning king with great strength,
possessor of the seven gems. Verse 4

Walt 2599 The four analytical modes,
and these eight deliverances,
six special knowledges mastered,
I have done what the Buddha taught! Verse 5

Thus indeed Venerable Abbhañjanadāyaka Thera spoke these verses.

The legend of Abbhañjanadāyaka Thera is finished.
