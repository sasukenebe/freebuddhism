Buddha Chapter
10. Question on Pointing Out the Buddha
The king asked: “Venerable Nāgasena, does the Buddha exist?” “Yes, your majesty, the Blessed One exists.” “Is it then possible, venerable sir, to point to the Buddha as being either here or there?” “The Blessed One has passed away, your majesty, in the element of nibbāna without residue remaining, so it is not possible to point to the Blessed One as being either here or there.”
“Please give me an analogy.” “What do you think, your majesty, when the flame of a great burning mass of fire has been extinguished, is it possible to point to that flame and say it remains here or there?” “Certainly not, venerable sir, for that flame has ceased and gone beyond description.” “In the same way, your majesty, the Blessed One has passed away in the element of nibbāna without residue remaining, so it is not possible to point to the Blessed One as being either here or there. However, your majesty, it is possible to point out the Blessed One through the body of Dhamma, for this Dhamma has been taught by the Blessed One.”
“You are clever, Venerable Nāgasena!”
