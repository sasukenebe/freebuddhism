Milinda’s Questions
Buddha Chapter
6. Question on the Experiencer
The king asked: “Venerable Nāgasena, is an experiencer to be found?” The elder responded: “According to ultimate reality, an experiencer is not to be found.”
“You are clever, Venerable Nāgasena!”
