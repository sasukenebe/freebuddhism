Verses of the Elder Bhikkhunīs
Chapter of the Ones
Verses of the Elder Dhīrā
“Dhīrā, touch cessation,
the joyful pacification of perception.
Attain Nirvana,
the utmost safety from bondage.”
The Elder Dhīrā
