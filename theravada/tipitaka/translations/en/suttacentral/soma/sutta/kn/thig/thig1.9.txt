Verses of the Elder Bhikkhunīs
Chapter of the Ones
Verses of the Elder Bhadrā
“Now that you have gone forth out of faith,
Bhadrā, be devoted to goodness.
Cultivate wholesome qualities,
the utmost safety from bondage.”
The Elder Bhadrā
