The Preference Chapter
One who is dedicated to that which is not worthy of dedication,
and who is not dedicated to that which is worthy of dedication,
has dropped the goal and grabbed their preferences -
yet envies those who are intent upon self-development.
Don’t get caught up in what you prefer
or in what you do not prefer.
Not seeing what you prefer is hard to bear
and so is seeing what you do not prefer.
Therefore don’t form any preference;
loss of what one prefers is painful.
One who has no attachments
has no preferences.
Sorrow is born from preference,
fear is born from preference;
one who is free from preference
has no sorrow and no fear.
Sorrow is born from affection,
fear is born from affection;
one who is free from affection
has no sorrow and no fear.
Sorrow is born from partiality,
fear is born from partiality;
one who is free from partiality
has no sorrow and no fear.
Sorrow is born from sensuality,
fear is born from sensuality;
one who is free from sensuality
has no sorrow and no fear.
Sorrow is born from craving,
fear is born from craving;
one who is free from craving
has no sorrow and no fear.
One who has perfect morality and wisdom,
who is established in the Dhamma and understands truth,
and who works on themself -
this is the kind of person that the populace prefers.
Interested in the inexpressible,
with an expanded mind,
and not bound by sensuality -
such a one is said be going upstream.
When a person who has been away for a long time
arrives safely from afar,
their relatives, friends, and loved ones
delight in that arrival.
In the same way, when one who has made merit
goes from this world to the next,
they are received by their merit
like the arrival of a beloved relative.
