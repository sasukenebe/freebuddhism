The Contaminant Chapter
Now you are like a fallen leaf,
and Death's minions are near.
You stand at the starting point of a struggle,
and no provisions can be found for you.
Make an island for yourself,
quickly try to become wise;
one who has no contaminants and no flaws
will reach the divine state of the noble ones.
Now you are close to departing;
you are in the presence of Death.
You have nowhere to dwell here,
and no provisions can be found for you.
Make an island for yourself,
quickly try to become wise;
one who has no contaminants and no flaws
does not come again to birth and decrepitude.
Progressively,
bit by bit, moment after moment,
like a smith purifying silver,
the wise remove their contaminants.
When rust appears
it eats away the metal that produced it;
in the same way, a sanctimonious person
is led to a bad destination by their own actions.
Non-repetition contaminates mantras,
non-upkeep contaminates a house,
laziness contaminates beauty,
negligence contaminates security.
Misconduct contaminates a woman,
stinginess contaminates a donor,
evil choices contaminate
both this life and the next.
There is a contaminant that is even worse than that:
ignorance, the paramount contaminant.
Abandon this contaminant
and be free of contaminants, monks!
It is easy to lose one's conscience,
to act like an impudent and offensive crow,
obtrusive and inconsiderate,
living a defiled life.
It is not easy to maintain one's conscience,
always seeking to be impeccable,
one who is diligent and considerate,
insightful and living a life of purity.
One who kills,
lies,
steals,
commits adultery,
and drinks alcohol -
a person who engages in such activities
right here in this world
destroys their own foundation.
Therefore, you should know
that lack of restraint is evil;
do not let greed contrary to Dhamma
afflict you with long-lasting suffering.
People give because it brings
faith and joy.
One who is disturbed
when food and drink are given to others
does not gain concentration
by day and by night.
One who has shredded,
uprooted, and destroyed that attitude
gains concentration
by day and by night.
There is no fire as strong as passion,
no grasp as strong as aversion,
no trap as strong as delusion,
no river as strong as craving.
It is easy to see the faults of others,
and hard to see our own.
When we frequently expose
the faults of others
but conceal our own,
we are like a fraud cheating at dice.
When one looks at the faults of others
and is always looking to criticize,
one's defilements grow
and one is far the elimination of defilements.
There is no path in the sky,
there is no contemplative elsewhere.
People delight in complication,
whereas a Truth-Finder is free of complication.
There is no path in the sky,
there is no contemplative elsewhere.
There is nothing that is eternal,
and no agitation in Buddhas.
