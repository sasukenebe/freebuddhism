The section on the classes of offenses in the Chapters, the first, is finished.
This is the summary:
“Full ordination, observance day,
Entering the rainy-season residence, invitation ceremony;
Skins, medicines, robe-making ceremony,
Robes, and with legal procedures,
The chapter on division in the Sangha, legal procedures,
The proper conduct for those who have committed an offense entailing suspension, legal procedures in relation to offenses entailing suspension;
Settling of legal issues, minor topics, resting places,
Schism in the Sangha, proper conduct;
Cancellation of the Monastic code, the chapter on nuns,
And with the first council and the second council.”
